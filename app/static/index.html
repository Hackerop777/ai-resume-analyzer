Our Architecture Philosophy

Why Low-Token Speed Matters More Than Endless Prompts

Most AI resume tools dump entire multi-page resumes and 2,000-word job descriptions repeatedly into slow LLM calls. This burns thousands of tokens, bloats response latency to 15+ seconds, and introduces hallucinated formatting errors.

Our approach is fundamentally different: Local Deterministic Pre-Processing handles ATS formatting, metric counting, and keyword intersection in 5 milliseconds. Only targeted, distilled evidence retrieved from ChromaDB is passed to Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite.

The result is an ultra-fast, sub-3 second turnaround with strict structured outputs, zero conversational fluff, and persistent short-term memory for intelligent follow-up interview prep.
